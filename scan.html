    About

    What this is. A companion to back-stop that reads the QR codes on a printed backup, puts the sheets together, and gives you one block of text to paste into back-stop. It exists for anyone who would rather not point a phone scanner app at their backup: this page does the reading itself, in this tab, and has no network access to send anything with.

    How to use it. Press Start camera and hold each printed sheet up in turn, in any order. Each time a code is read the sheet is ticked off. When every sheet is in, the joined text appears. Copy it, go back to the back-stop tab, choose From paper, paste it and choose Read backup. You can also add photos or screenshots of the codes instead of using the camera.

    Why a separate page. The reader is the one part of back-stop that has to look at the world, so it is kept out of the page that holds your codes. This page only ever sees the encrypted backup, which is useless without your passphrase or key card. It does not know your passphrase, and it cannot open a vault. The two pages do not talk to each other: the text is handed over by you, through the clipboard, on purpose.

    Getting a good read. Light the sheet evenly and keep glare off it. Hold it flat and fill most of the view with the square. A dense code, with many small squares, needs a steadier hand and a sharper camera than a small one: move closer, or use Zoom if it appears, and let the camera focus. If a code will not read, take a photo with any camera and add it instead, or open the printed copy in another light.

    The recovery key card. Its code is read the same way. The key is checked as it is read, using the same two check characters as the card, so a bad read is caught here and not later. It is shown blurred and has no automatic copy.

    Where your data goes. Nowhere. This page cannot connect to the internet. The camera is used only while the camera is on: it stops when every sheet is in, when you press Stop, and when you leave the tab. Nothing is saved in the browser except your choice of design, mode and accent, and everything scanned is cleared after five minutes idle.

    If it cannot read a code. Any other QR scanner can read these codes: each one holds a line of text beginning BS1, and the lines can be pasted into back-stop in any order. Or use recover.py from the same repository, which opens a scanned backup with nothing but Python.

    How it reads a code. The decoder is written for this tool and is in this file. It finds the three corner marks, works out the size, straightens the grid using the smaller alignment marks, reads each square, and repairs misread squares with the code's built-in error correction. A code is accepted only when every block corrects cleanly.
